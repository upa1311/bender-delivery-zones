# Stage 09C — Borisovka via the south (route №5) corridor

**Read-only. No OSM edit, no release, no Direct, no price, no new zone.
owner_review_required.**

Owner asked whether the same southern corridor (…Главана → Старого → Юг-2 →
Борисовский рынок) also shortens Borisovka. Tested over a Borisovka sample
(`docs/data/stage-09c-borisovka-south-corridor.csv`):

- **0 / 43** Borisovka homes are shortened by forcing the **south** brewery
  corridor versus the shortest route.

Borisovka's real shortcut is the **NORTH Кишинёв–Тирасполь путепровод**
(`reports/stage-09c/borisovka-corridor.md`), not the south crossing — the OSRM
fastest route to Borisovka already takes the south loop, which is the LONGER of
the two corridors. So the south corridor confirms car connectivity but does not
help Borisovka; the north путепровод does (168/511 homes cheaper if the shortest
route's km is used). owner_review; no zone changed.
